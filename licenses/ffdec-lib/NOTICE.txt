openDoJa uses its Java timeline/vector/bitmap rendering. ActionScript execution,
Flash network callbacks and audio/video playback are not implemented by this bridge.
The library can be replaced by changing the jpexs-decompiler dependency in pom.xml
and rebuilding openDoJa with Maven.
